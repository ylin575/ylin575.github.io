Meyer Lab, Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory

seq-to-flow Ongoing

Computational workflow · R

A workflow that turns single-cell RNA-seq data into flow cytometry panel recommendations: which surface markers best separate the populations of interest, so that clusters found by sequencing can be sorted or tracked by cytometry.
